## Naming Conventions

- **Namespaces:** match the project; internal helpers in `Moberg.EntityFrameworkCore.DesignerlessMigrations.Scaffolding` (never `*.Internal`, which trips EF1001). The options extension lives in `Microsoft.EntityFrameworkCore` so it needs no extra using.
- **Tests:** classes `{ClassUnderTest}Tests` (partial classes split by method, e.g. `DesignerlessMigrationsScaffolderTests.RemoveMigration.cs`); methods `Method_WhenCondition_ThenResult` (67 of 68; the README-mirroring sample test is the exception).

- `src/<Package>/` — public types at the root, internal helpers in `Scaffolding/`, MSBuild assets in `build/`.
- `tests/<Project>/` — `Fixture/` for EF fixtures (context, migrations, snapshot).
